About

A teaching case beside a working hospital

The checker is a proposed application. King Faisal Hospital Rwanda is the setting used to think about where it would fit.

The hospital

King Faisal Hospital Rwanda is a specialized referral and teaching hospital in Kigali. It provides healthcare to patients from Rwanda and from other countries in the region. Day-to-day operations run through an Integrated Health Management Information System, which keeps patient information together and helps departments coordinate. Public reporting describes that system as linking laboratory, radiology, medical imaging, and patient services (The New Times, 2025).

The project

The AI-Based Symptom Checker & Lifestyle Advisor lets a person enter symptoms, receive preliminary guidance, and read general advice about nutrition, movement, and daily habits. Administrators manage user information and reports. A healthcare professional, once authorized, can review symptom checks.

The word “AI” in the project title describes the job the software is standing in for: intake, a first sort of urgency, and lifestyle guidance. The version in this case study uses written safety rules, not a diagnostic model. The rules are visible in what the result tells you, and they refuse to invent a disease name.

What a result is

  • A care level: home care for now, arrange a review, get care today, or seek emergency help.
  • A short note on everyday patterns those symptoms can appear with.
  • Practical next steps, including when to stop waiting.

What a result is not

  • A diagnosis, a prescription, or a reading of your tests and images.
  • A replacement for the hospital’s IHMIS or for the clinician in front of you.
  • Advice that is safe to follow if you are getting worse. Emergency symptoms need 912 or 112.

Who uses which part

Patient

Registers, runs symptom checks, saves lifestyle plans, and updates their own profile.

Healthcare professional

An administrator can mark an account as staff. Staff can read symptom checks. They do not manage passwords or roles.

Administrator

The built-in desk account manages users, reads messages, and generates reports. Its sign-in is predefined for this case study.

How accounts are stored

Name, username, email, phone, and a hashed password are stored in MySQL on this server, along with symptom checks and lifestyle plans. Symptom checks are visible to you, to administrators, and to authorized professionals. Lifestyle plans stay with you and the administrator. This is a local case study, not a production privacy programme.
