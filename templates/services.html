Services

Guidance before the appointment, not instead of it

Two tools for people using the case study, and a review desk for authorized staff.

For you

Symptom checker

Choose symptoms from grouped lists, then add duration, severity, and age group. The rules look first for emergency signs: chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, confusion, and a few phrases in your note that should never wait.

Everything else is phrased as a possibility. A cold-like pattern stays in home-care language. A child, a possible pregnancy, or a symptom that has lingered is moved toward a clinical review.

Open the checker

For daily life

Lifestyle advisor

Pick a focus such as energy, sleep, weight, heart health, stress, or recovery while unwell. Add how active you are, how you eat, and how much you sleep. The plan that comes back is ordinary public-health advice: meals, walking, sleep timing, and a few cautions.

It will not set a crash diet, a supplement dose, or a training load.

Build a plan

For the desk

Accounts, review, and reports

Administrators create staff accounts, disable a user, reset a password, read contact messages, and export symptom checks. Healthcare professionals open the review list and filter by care level.
