Not a diagnosis. This application sorts common symptoms with safety rules and offers general lifestyle information. It does not examine you, read scans, or replace King Faisal Hospital.

What you can do

Three parts of the same visit

01

Symptom check

Select what you feel, how long it has lasted, and how much it is getting in the way. The result is a care level: home care, a booked review, same-day care, or emergency.

02

Lifestyle advisor

Get plain guidance on meals, walking, sleep, and everyday habits. Plans stay general. They are not a personal diet or a training prescription.

03

Staff review

Administrators manage accounts and reports. A healthcare professional authorized by the administrator can read symptom checks when follow-up is needed.

How a check works

Four steps, then a person if you need one

  1. Create an accountName, username, email, and phone are checked before the account is saved.
  2. Describe the symptomsPick from a set list and add a short note if something important is missing.
  3. Read the guidanceYou get a care level, what the pattern can be associated with, and what to do next.
  4. Keep the recordYour checks stay on your account. Authorized staff can review them.

The setting

A referral hospital that already runs on shared records

King Faisal Hospital Rwanda is a specialized referral and teaching hospital in Kigali. People come from Rwanda and from other countries in the region. The hospital uses an Integrated Health Management Information System to run daily work, keep patient information together, and coordinate departments. That system already links laboratory, radiology, medical imaging, and patient services.

This project uses the hospital as a case study for a narrower question: how a symptom checker and lifestyle advisor could sit beside those services, without pretending to be the clinical record.
